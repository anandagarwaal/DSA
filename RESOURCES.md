# DSA Interview Prep Resources

## Knowledge

- [NeetCode 150 — roadmap & video solutions](https://neetcode.io/practice)
  The gold-standard curated problem set, grouped by pattern. Use for: which
  problems to drill per topic, and clear video walkthroughs when stuck.
- [LeetCode](https://leetcode.com)
  Primary practice platform. Use for: drilling problems, the discuss tab for
  alternative approaches, and company-tagged question lists (Google, Meta).
- [cp-algorithms.com](https://cp-algorithms.com/)
  Rigorous, correct algorithm reference. Use for: the *why* behind an algorithm
  (binary search, graphs, DP, number theory) when interview-depth understanding is needed.
- [Sean Prashad — Leetcode Patterns](https://seanprashad.com/leetcode-patterns/)
  Problems grouped by pattern with difficulty. Use for: pattern-based interleaved practice.
- [Tech Interview Handbook — Algorithms](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/algorithms/study-cheatsheet/)
  Concise per-topic cheatsheets and "things to look out for". Use for: pre-interview review.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/leetcode](https://reddit.com/r/leetcode)
  High-volume community for interview experiences and problem discussion. Use for:
  recent Google/Meta interview reports, sanity-checking difficulty.
- [LeetCode Discuss](https://leetcode.com/discuss/interview-question)
  Use for: real tagged interview questions and idiomatic solution critique.
