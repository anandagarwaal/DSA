Debrief — how Staff candidates are judged

At L6/L7, getting a working solution is table stakes. The signal is everything around it: naming the pattern fast, stating why it's correct and why it's optimal (the exchange argument, the recurrence, the invariant), giving tight complexity bounds, and noting the better specialised alternative when one exists (bucket sort over a heap, quickselect over sorting, binary-search-on-answer over a heap). Meta weights clean, fast, bug-free code; Google weights the "why." This course trained the recognition; the remaining work is volume and the verbal articulation — narrate your reasoning out loud as you solve.
