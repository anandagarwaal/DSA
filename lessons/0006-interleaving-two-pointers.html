Lesson 0006 · Interleaving

Mixed Retrieval — Hashing + Two Pointers

Now the two patterns compete. The skill is choosing correctly under a cold read.

Do this after a 2–3 day gap from Lessons 0004–0005. You now own two tools that often apply to the same-looking problem — pair sums, in-place edits, dedup. Telling them apart fast is exactly the interview skill.

The decision fork to internalise

SignalReach for
Unsorted + need original indicesHash map (value → index)
Sorted + want O(1) extra spaceConverging two pointers
"Have I seen it?" / counts / group-byHash set / map
In-place rewrite / dedup / partitionFast/slow pointers
Subarray sum with negativesPrefix sum + map
Pair/triplet on sortable data, no index needSort + two pointers

Drill — name the tool, cold

Say the approach and its complexity in under 30 seconds, then check.

Debrief

The recurring trap: a problem "smells" like a hash map, but a sort + two pointers is cheaper in space — or vice versa, where sorting would destroy the indices you must return. Verbalising that tradeoff is the Staff-level signal.
