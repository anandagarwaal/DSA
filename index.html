DSA for L6/L7 Interviews

Rebuilding pattern fluency for Google & Meta Staff-level coding rounds — in Java.

The mission. Convert senior engineering experience into fluent, communicated problem-solving on medium–hard problems under a 35–45 min clock. Recognise the pattern in 2–3 minutes, code it cleanly, and justify why it's correct and optimal. 3–6 month runway · mix style · spaced & interleaved.

How this course works

Each pattern moves through a fixed three-step cadence. Don't skip — the order is the method:

  1. Lesson — short concept, then drills with feedback (builds the model).
  2. More problems — extra reps in the same pattern (builds fluency).
  3. Interleaving — mixed, blind retrieval across patterns you've learned, done after a 2–3 day gap (builds durable recognition — the real interview skill).

Then the next pattern begins, and its interleaving step folds in everything before it.
